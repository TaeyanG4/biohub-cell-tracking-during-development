## Facts that change how ideas must be judged
- **Hidden test = different embryos.** Host (Thibgolds, 716793): "test set roughly similar in size, with no overlap in embryo_ids between train and test"; host (Bragantini, 724386): same instrument/protocol/stage, each embryo a separate imaging session. Timmy Juicehouse probed the test size as <= 2/3 of train. Public LB = 29 % of the test; a public/private split along embryos is possible (Sangram's guess), so the private part may behave differently from the public one.
- **Local validation on train movies is in-sample for every public checkpoint** (Eric 742064, Mark_RowSet 730160). Mark_RowSet: ablating a corrective post-processing stage gained +0.0184 locally and lost 0.003 on the LB - corrective stages look useless on memorised movies and matter on unseen ones. Our own C015 (readmit off: local +, LB -) is the same effect. Mark (25th) and Yubo WANG: smarter motion linkers +0.01..0.03 CV, exactly +0.001 LB.
- Divisions: 151 GT divisions in 199 movies; 112 movies have none (Luka Duvanov 733973); the two train embryos differ a lot (annotation 1 % vs 9 %, divisions 26 vs 125; Ace in 732103). Tim Krige (138th): daughter/parent volume in GT is ~0.75 rather than 0.5 (GT division timing is late or partial).
- Motion: frame-to-frame displacement median 1.82 um, p95 5.34, p99 8.38 um (all 128,883 GT links). Frozen frames (byte-identical consecutive volumes) and sudden whole-volume jumps occur (hengck23 724283, g john rao 729082: 6bba_fc516dc6 t=81/82 identical, GT edge 8.9 um).
- Top players: Tang (3rd) - retrain the model, better preprocessing/architecture, the public checkpoint "has hit a wall"; Soheil Ayati (8th) - split missed edges into missing endpoint nodes vs wrong associations, many 'linking' errors start at node selection; Masha Mikhisor (7th) validates on external embryos; Sergio Alvarez (1st) tried synthetic volumes for two weeks with no clear gain.
