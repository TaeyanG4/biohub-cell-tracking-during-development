## What C061 rules out as the next shortcut

C061 learns its source artificial axial displacements, but both opposite
embryos fail at zero and +/-one-plane shifts. On real C023 anchors, mean3D
error rises1.781070 to2.781440um and20/22movies worsen. Even its own-source
real good-point means worsen. Consequently, adding source epochs, loosening a
guard, changing a displacement strength or continuing to optimize the pooled
synthetic average would target the wrong evidence. The new experiment must
use real inference anchors, preserve original known identities, and protect
the already-good majority in both embryo directions.

The public-source review found no unused verified drop-in correction. x138's
V1284 head is already part of C023: a frozen32-channel center plus six
neighbor differences,224-to32-to3 MLP, bounded2um displacement. A larger frozen
spatial input is distinct representation work, but not a wholly new public
mechanism. Other available detector trainers use unknown-location penalties
or within-embryo splits, and DeepCenter's historical coordinate contract
remains unresolved.

## Preferred quick test: three-observation trajectory identity

Use the immutable opposite-embryo C048 appearance encoder; do not refit it.
On the two registered full movies44b6_12dfb391 and6bba_05db0fb1, reuse C023/C038's
actual candidate groups and graph/context protections. Build a source
three-node history ending at t and each child's three-node future starting at
t+1. Average their existing normalized32-dimensional embeddings, normalize
that average, and take the prototype cosine. There is exactly one formula and
window length, with no score weight or margin search.

Score the exact same groups with the same checkpoint's single-frame cosine
as the paired control. Reject missing, synthetic, forked or ambiguous predicted
contexts using inference-available topology/support only. Do not select nodes
by GT error or construct histories from GT tracks. Cache every requested
prediction's embedding once. Duplicate-vector prototypes must reproduce the
single-frame score, and enabling passive context collection must leave C023
nodes/edges and the original single-frame diagnostic unchanged.

Use only known endpoint identities to establish ranking rescues versus harms.
Unknown alternatives remain reported as unknown, not false biological cells.
Report all eligible groups, the strict labelled subset, unknown-choice burden,
and unique GT link identities represented, so several overlapping windows do
not masquerade as independent recovery. Excluded groups retain the original
decision. Compare on an identical common support, not different easy subsets.

**Fixed decision:** require positive net known-link rescues over harms on each
embryo, actual rescues on both, and no increase in choosing known-distinct
targets under the unchanged existing C048 admission rules. Report whether
the same examples improve over both single-frame appearance and baseline
geometry. A result based only on larger cosine margins, pure coverage changes,
or unknown choices does not pass. If strict labelled coverage contains no
actual mistakes to rescue, call the probe inconclusive and stop; do not adjust
the window or margins to manufacture a positive result.

A pass can justify a separately fixed all22 replay through existing
collision-safe reciprocal/unused-target editing, with exact off controls.
Require actual official edge TP and adjusted edge improvements on both embryos,
no division regression, positive total overall, and gains in at least two
movies per embryo before unchanged97 review. Those counts protect against a
single matched identity exchange being described as broad recovery. No source
or target coordinates change, so this path directly addresses scored edges
without rebuilding the detector, feature indexing or ILP.

**Why this avoids the most direct C061 mismatch:** training and runtime both
use the same frozen appearance representation and actual predicted nodes;
there are no generated displacements and no new absolute-center regression.
Temporal averaging can reduce a single frame's appearance noise. It does not
remove encoder domain bias, and a wrong predicted track can contaminate every
prototype. C048's own97 result was small/mixed, so this is a signal test for
new temporal information, not promotion of that closed single-frame arm.

## Localization alternative: real-anchor frozen spatial evidence

The minimum defensible change is to replace the raw scratch-trained image
encoder with **exact production frozen UNet spatial neighborhoods at real
C023 centers**, while preserving the backbone, public V1284 correction,
detector, edge Transformer, normalization state and graph. Do not train on
synthetic shifted GT centers, replace the existing head with another224-vector
MLP, or tune a scalar on its output.

The first capture should use the same two movies and prove a passive no-op:
the exact original execution's full-movie nodes, edges and official outputs
must be unchanged. Reuse the V1284 capture hook and established frame reader.
Capture spatial feature values from the real temporal window/first-seen frame
path, not an independently centered small-patch UNet call; the latter changes
context/padding and would recreate a representation mismatch. Record the
feature lattice origin,1.625um grid, original continuous/final integer center
conversion and any boundary exclusions explicitly.

**Small fixed trainable protocol, if capture is practical:**

- Train only an auxiliary shared local-position scoring head on known real
  C023 prediction-to-GT pairs; keep both the original node and GT IDs fixed.
  Use whole-embryo opposite fits and all original good/tail strata, with uniform
  source movie then known-pair sampling. No deliberate class balancing or
  artificial displacement augmentation: real-anchor residual frequencies are
  the deployment task here.
- Use a physical candidate lattice covering the existing7um matching radius
  and a spatial feature neighborhood with real support around it. A shared
  scorer applied to each candidate's pretrained local feature neighborhood
  avoids separate freely learned class intercepts or an embryo-prefix input.
  A local center-feature difference/contrast representation reuses V1284's
  idea without global target-domain standardization. Freeze its precise extent,
  head and normalization after capture controls, before seeing fit results.
  This is a design boundary, not an approved architecture sweep.
- Targets are the conditional location of the supplied known point; do not
  introduce a whole-frame cell/background loss. For fractional lateral offsets,
  preserve exact coordinates with an explicit continuous or interpolated-label
  target. Do not round labels solely to make a class table convenient.
- Initialize the output to a unique declared no-op fallback on tied uniform
  logits. Fit one final-checkpoint-only schedule in each embryo; a small
  fixed1200-step, batch32, lr3e-4 AdamW recipe can reuse existing infrastructure.
  Source training may fit better without transferring; both must be reported.

This shared-scoring design removes one route for memorizing a constant class
preference, but **does not guarantee elimination of embryo bias**: frozen
features can still encode illumination, anatomy and the old detector's errors.
It also returns to matched-pair label ambiguity. No bias subtraction using the
opposite embryo, prefix-specific deployment routing, or evaluated decoder
selection is acceptable.

**Localization acceptance:** on actual anchors in both opposite directions,
require lower mean3D and absolute z errors overall, on >3.5um3D tails and on
|dz|>3.5um tails; no worsening of originally<=2.5um means. Include all no-ops
and excluded-original pairs, signed bias, per-movie effects and proposed
ownership conflicts. Use original identities, without correcting the labels
through rematching. Require tail wins on more than one movie per embryo.
Before any scored integration, actual all-node inference must preserve good
known identities under the original matcher, and exact zero controls must
match the original writer and official evaluator.

Fixed-final-graph residual improvements do not create missing edges when GT
assignments stay unchanged. A pre-association gain requires fresh feature
indexing, candidates, Transformer probabilities and ILP at changed coordinates.
That is a materially longer integration path than fixed-node trajectory
appearance. The frozen backbone saw both embryos; opposite-embryo evidence
validates the new head's transfer conditional on that frozen encoder, not a
fully unseen-embryo detector.
